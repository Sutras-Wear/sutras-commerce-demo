A FEW LITTLE DETAILS

Let’s make it easy.

Have something else in mind?
We’re only a message away.

How do I place an order?
Say hello on WhatsApp using either number below. Tell us what you’re looking for, or add a style preview to your enquiry bag and send it to us. We’ll confirm the real pieces, prices, sizes and order details with you. Sending an enquiry does not place or reserve an order.
Are these the current products?
Our product listings are based on photographs supplied by Sutras. Product pages currently show the modelled view only, which illustrates styling rather than exact fit. Please confirm current availability, prices and garment measurements on WhatsApp before ordering.
What sizes are available?
Please ask us about sizing for the piece you love. You can include your usual size in a WhatsApp enquiry, but we’ll need to confirm the fit, measurements and available sizes for each actual garment.
What about delivery and payment?
We’re based in Lusaka, Zambia. Contact us to confirm delivery or collection options, delivery fees, timing and accepted payment methods before ordering. This website doesn’t take payments or automatically confirm orders.

A note on this site.

Imagery & the collection

AI model views use the actual garment designs from the collection, but the model and photography are AI-generated. Fit, length, drape and small details are approximate. Additional product photographs have been taken out of the galleries while better replacements are prepared. The campaign and moodboard also use illustrative imagery. Please confirm the real garment details before ordering.

Orders & payments

WhatsApp links open a conversation with Sutras. No payment, order or reservation is completed by this website. Please confirm all product, payment, delivery and return details with the store before purchasing.

Your privacy

Your enquiry bag and optional note are stored only in this browser when local storage is available. There are no analytics, tracking pixels, newsletter forms or payment processors on this site. Clear your bag to remove its saved contents. Messages are passed to WhatsApp only when you follow a WhatsApp link. Instagram, WhatsApp and GitHub Pages operate under their own privacy policies.
